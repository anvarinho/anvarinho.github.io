# Product imagery provenance

The GuideBook images are genuine browser captures of https://central-asia.live/en/places taken on 22 October 2026:

- `guidebook-web-1200.webp` and `guidebook-web-720.webp`: derived from a 1440 × 1000 desktop capture.
- `guidebook-mobile-web.webp`: a 390 × 844 capture of the responsive **website**, not an iOS or Android app.

Capture source and original PNGs are retained locally in `/tmp/portfolio-upgrade-review/`. The files were resized/encoded without inventing or replacing product UI. No verified native-app or WeCan screenshot was found in the inspected material. The existing GuideBook mountain and WeCan orbit artwork remain explicitly labeled project illustrations in the case studies.
